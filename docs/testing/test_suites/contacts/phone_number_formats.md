## Enter a phone number in national format

### Preconditions

- Use controlled numbers with a known numbering plan; record SIM country and locale.

### Steps

1. Open new conversation recipient selection.
2. Enter a phone number in national format.
3. Check the query, Send to destination and any matched contact before confirming; do not send to uncontrolled numbers.

### Expected Result

- The entered destination is not silently changed to a different subscriber; record formatting, validation and matching behavior.

Draft: confirm the expected result against the implementation and on a test device before release testing.
